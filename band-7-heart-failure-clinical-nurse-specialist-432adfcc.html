Full job description

We are excited to offer an excellent opportunity for an experienced and enthusiastic HF CNS to join our heart failure service for a 12 month fixed term/secondment as a Band 7 Heart Failure CNS. This post offers the chance to be part of a proactive, multidisciplinary team that delivers high-quality, patient-centred care across both hospital and outpatient settings. You will be based at Heartlands, Goodhope and Solihull Hospitals and play a key role in the assessment, management, and ongoing support of adults living with heart failure.
