Full job description

Whether you are a Ward Manager/ Head of department or experienced Nurse, if you are looking for the next step in your career then the Head of Clinical Services could be just what you are looking for. You will join the team at the Avonfield Neurological Centre and experience what delivering great healthcare feels like. As an experienced Nurse from the NHS or Private Care, you will provide clinical management and leadership to the unit team; acting as a role model within the service to foster a culture of professional development and excellent standards of care, promote positive team building and set priorities, objectives and goals. Your responsibilities will include: Responsible for providing clinical direction and strong, professional leadership to all clinical staff including nursing, allied health professionals and medical staff either directly employed or working under practising privileges and in line with Elysium policy and national clinical guidance. Lead the clinical services to deliver a high level of governance, safe effective patient care and identify and reduce clinical risk. The Head of Clinical Services at Elysium Healthcare's Avonfield Neurological Centre is a pivotal role for those ready for the next step in their clinical career. This opportunity is ideal for an experienced Nurse, Ward Manager, or Head of Department seeking new challenges. The role involves providing clinical management and leadership, fostering a culture of professional development, and ensuring excellent standards of care. Key responsibilities include leading clinical services, reducing clinical risk, and supporting the Service Director with business development and safe care delivery. The position also demands active contribution to successful service risk mitigation and monitoring and delivering on the facility's clinical strategy and annual plan. Strong communication and interpersonal skills are crucial for championing change and innovation within the team, alongside a solid understanding of CQC legislation and standards. Elysium Healthcare is a leading provider of care, committed to offering innovative healthcare services. The company employs over 8,000 staff and has a growing network of more than 80 services across England and Wales, covering various specializations such as Mental Health, Neurological, Learning Disabilities & Autism, and Children & Education services. Elysium Healthcare is part of Ramsay Health Care, a global organization with operations spanning 10 countries and a workforce of over 86,000 individuals. Committed to inclusive recruitment practices, Elysium Healthcare is a Disability Confident employer. Through its comprehensive benefits, staff are supported in their wellbeing, growth, and future within a stable and agile company that values professional development and continuous quality improvement, ensuring everyone can thrive while delivering exceptional care services. Whether you are a Ward Manager/ Head of department or experienced Nurse, if you are looking for the next step in your career then the Head of Clinical Services could be just what you are looking for.
