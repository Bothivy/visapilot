Full job description

Manage front of house reception areas. Deliver excellent customer service. Communicate effectively, in a calm, professional and empathetic way. Produce work accurately under pressure in a busy environment. Have good keyboard skills and the ability to learn and use clinical and administrative systems. Prioritise tasks and working effectively as part of a team. Have a flexible approach to managing your workload and service requirements. Thameside Medical Practice is a small, friendly GP surgery located in Teddington, South West London. We look after the health needs of approximately 5,500 local patients and proudly participate in providing training for the future GP workforce. We have been serving Teddington and the surrounding area for more than 30 years and are located in a purpose built building. There are 3 GP Partners, 3 salaried doctors, a nurse practitioner, clinical pharmacist, nurse associate and healthcare assistant. We also have a small team of receptionists and administrators with a structure which lends itself to career progression. The post holder will work with colleagues to ensure the highest standards of care for all patients, dealing with a wide range of administrative and management tasks in a general primary healthcare practice setting.
