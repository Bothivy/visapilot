Full job description

Salisbury NHS Foundation Trust is an innovative and dynamic place to work. Our values - Patient Centred and Safe, Professional, Responsive and Friendly - are at the core of our practice and care and are the basis for our interactions with patients and each other. Our ideal candidate will be confident with appropriate NHS ward based experience, clearly able to demonstrate a desire to participate in all aspects of the role. The role will be ward based which will require the candidate to work with their allocated patient caseload to facilitate all aspects of their stroke rehabilitation. This will include progressing and following physical and cognitive rehabilitation plans that have been issued by their allocated physiotherapist or occupational therapist and some group work. There will also be an expectation to lead on basic discharge conversations. Salisbury NHS Foundation Trust (SFT) is an innovative hospital with a proud heritage and over 250 years of experience behind us. Here we're driven to deliver an outstanding experience for everyone and have been well regarded for the quality of care and treatment we continue to provide for our patients and also our staff.
