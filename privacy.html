3. How and why we use it

We process information to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • match your profile and CV to vacancies;
  • provide employer-level sponsor-licence information separately from vacancy-level sponsorship evidence;
  • generate CV reviews, tailored CVs, cover letters and application or interview preparation;
  • save your documents, applications and preferences;
  • manage subscriptions and prevent fraud or misuse;
  • provide support, maintain security and improve reliability; and
  • meet legal, accounting and regulatory requirements.

Our principal lawful bases are performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing VisaPilot, and compliance with legal obligations. Where legally required for an optional activity such as direct marketing, we will ask for consent.

4. AI and recommendations

VisaPilot uses automated tools to analyse job information and information you provide. Outputs are recommendations intended to support your decisions. They do not guarantee sponsorship, employment, an interview or a successful visa application, and they do not make legally significant decisions about you.

5. Who receives information

We use carefully selected providers to operate VisaPilot, including hosting/database and authentication providers, payment provider Stripe, email/support providers, job-data providers and AI-processing providers. They may process only the information needed to provide their services, subject to applicable contracts and safeguards. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a properly documented business transfer.

When you choose to apply to an employer, information is shared with that employer or its recruitment provider only as needed for the route you select or authorise.

6. International transfers

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where required, we use recognised safeguards such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contractual protections.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account, profile and candidate documents: while your account is active and normally deleted or anonymised within 90 days after a valid account-deletion request, subject to backups and legal requirements.
  • Support and security records: normally up to 24 months.
  • Payment and accounting records: normally six years where required for tax and accounting obligations.
  • De-identified service statistics may be retained where they can no longer identify you.

We may retain particular records for longer where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, prevent fraud or comply with law.

8. Security

We use access controls, private document storage, encrypted connections and technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password confidential and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.
