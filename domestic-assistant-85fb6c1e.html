We are recruiting Domestic Assistants to join our team at Carleton Clinic, Cumbria. These posts will be offered at Part Time, 30 hours per week on a rolling rota to cover a 7-day service. Carrying out a range of cleaning tasks in areas such as offices, public areas such as corridors, areas which patients use such as shared spaces on wards as well as bedrooms and bathrooms Cleaning tasks can include things like dusting of walls, ceilings and curtain rails, dusting and polishing of furniture and paintwork, hoovering of carpets, mopping floors Being able to follow instructions and follow a routine work schedule which sets out your tasks and carry out those duties often without supervision Being aware of the patients that you will be working alongside and work in a way that is respectful, caring and compassionate You will use equipment and cleaning products safely, adhering to all control of substances harmful to health (COSHH) rules and regulations Understanding infection prevention and control rules and adhering to them Completing any paperwork such as quality checks and any other documentation required Help with any cleaning audits or other surveys and inspections that take place Taking part in any training that you need to do and keep up to date with all company communications You will be respectful of other people and work in a way that supports equality, diversity and inclusion You will demonstrate our company values our values of being caring and compassionate, respectful, and honest and transparent in all that you do NTW Solutions Limited is proud to provide services to the NHS. We keep hospitals clean and patients fed and we keep buildings running and grounds tidy. Our digital teams support the vital systems the NHS relies on, our pharmacy team ensures patients get the medicine they need, and our workforce team recruits people onto the frontline. We transcribe thousands of important medical letters, provide a lease car service to keep people on the move, and ensure suppliers are paid on time.
