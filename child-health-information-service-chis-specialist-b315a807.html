Full job description

The CHIS Specialist post requires inputting data and carrying out crucial data quality checks that support the Child Health service outputs and following systems that promote & support the continued development and improvement of data quality across Child Health services. Whilst also developing expertise in CHIS IT systems (CHISS) and providing support to the preparation and presentation of data and reports. The post holder will be responsible for maintaining accurate and up to date Child health data records and office management systems. Ensuring Child Health data is effectively processed and quality assured in accordance with NHS policies and procedures to facilitate timely and accurate reporting of Child Health activity and monitoring performance. At NCIC, we have an ambition to deliver outstanding healthcare services to the population of North Cumbria. The Trust is committed to creating a centre of excellence in providing rural and remote healthcare and provides a wide range of community and acute services throughout our county.
